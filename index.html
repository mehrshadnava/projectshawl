Keep it soft

  • Wash
    Hand wash in cold water with a gentle wool or baby shampoo. No wringing or twisting.
  • Dry
    Roll it in a towel to press out water, then dry flat in the shade so it keeps its shape.
  • Hang?
    Don't hang a wet scarf. Knits stretch long when they're heavy with water.
  • Fuzz
    A little pilling at first is normal. Lift it gently with a wool comb.

Little questions

Is it itchy?

Merino and alpaca blend are soft enough for sensitive necks. Lambswool is cosy but can feel a little scratchy on bare skin.

Will the colour match the preview?

Close, but not exact. Screens show colours differently. We send you a photo of your yarn before knitting starts, so you can ask for a change.

Can I return a made-to-order scarf?

Each scarf is knitted just for you, so we can't take returns for a change of mind. If there's a dropped stitch or other fault, we fix it.

Can you knit a matching hat or a kids' size?

Yes! Write it in the notes when you order and we'll quote before starting.

How do I pay?

Once we confirm your design, we send a payment link. Knitting starts after the advance arrives.
